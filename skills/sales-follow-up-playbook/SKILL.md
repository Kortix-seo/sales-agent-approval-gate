# Sales follow-up playbook

This skill belongs to an open-source Kortix project: how this company works a new lead, as a file next to the agent that reads it.

## The research note

Four fields, in this order, each one line:

- **What they do.** One sentence a colleague would accept without asking.
- **Why now.** The signal on the record: the page they read, the plan they
  asked about, a hiring page, a launch. Quote it and link it.
- **Fit.** Which of our offerings this looks like, or that you cannot tell.
- **Open question.** The one thing the reply should ask.

## The reply

- Under 120 words.
- Name the thing they asked about in the first line.
- One question, the open one from the note. Not a list of qualifying questions.
- One call slot, a real one from the calendar, offered as a time, not a link to
  a booking page.
- No pricing in the first reply unless the form asked for it; pricing is a
  conversation, and a wrong number costs more than a slow one.

## The call slot

Offer the first slot that is free for the account owner and at least two hours
away. If no slot exists in the next three working days, say so in the note and
offer nothing rather than a slot nobody can take.

## Escalate instead of replying

Write no draft, and leave the record for a person, when:

- the lead is an existing customer or has an open support thread;
- the form mentions security review, procurement, legal or a data processing
  agreement;
- the lead is a competitor, a recruiter, or press;
- anything in the record contradicts the research.

## The daily summary

At the end of a sweep, one paragraph: leads worked, approvals waiting, leads
escalated and why. This goes in the session output, not into the CRM.
